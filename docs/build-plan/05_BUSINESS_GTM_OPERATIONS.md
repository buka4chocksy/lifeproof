## 1. Business thesis

LifeProof is a **verified-release infrastructure company** with a family-facing vault, sold through trusted intermediaries, with crypto as a premium partner-led module and concierge as the trust-building service.

The business is built on three truths:

1. **Families do not wake up wanting a vault.** They want peace of mind and a process that works when something happens.
2. **Trusted intermediaries own the relationship.** Solicitors, will-writers, insurers, brokers, accountants, exchanges, and employers already have the trust and the client base.
3. **The release is the hard part.** Storage is commodity; verification, legal acceptance, fraud prevention, and a humane beneficiary experience are scarce and defensible.

The revenue model follows the value:

- Families pay for **peace of mind and continuity**.
- Partners pay for **distribution, workflow, and verification infrastructure**.
- High-touch families and estates pay for **concierge and release services**.
- Crypto holders pay a premium for **partner-led continuity** without LifeProof holding secrets.

## 2. Market and competition

### 2.1 Market drivers

- Aging populations and rising inheritance complexity.
- Digital assets (crypto, accounts, subscriptions) are invisible to traditional estate planning.
- Probate delays and family friction create demand for readiness tools.
- Insurers, banks, and exchanges face support burden and fraud risk when a customer dies.
- Regulatory attention to digital assets and data protection is increasing.
- Trusted intermediaries need digital services to remain relevant.

### 2.2 Initial market choice

Choose **one** launch market for Phase 1:

| Option | Advantages | Challenges | Best if |
|---|---|---|---|
| **United Kingdom** | Clear probate, will-writing market, GDPR, insurer/broker channel, professional trust | Higher CAC, established competitors, slower sales | You have solicitor/insurer access |
| **Nigeria / Lagos + diaspora** | High crypto adoption, strong community trust, SME base, less competition | Regulatory uncertainty, weaker death registration, payment friction | You have community/SME access |
| **Australia** | Mature estate planning, crypto guidance | Smaller market, distance | Phase 3 |

**Recommendation:** choose based on network strength. If the founding team has Nigerian community/SME distribution, start in Lagos with a UK entity for trust and compliance. If the team has UK professional-channel access, start with UK will-writers/solicitors. Do not launch both simultaneously.

### 2.3 Competitive landscape

| Category | Examples | What they do well | Where LifeProof wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| Password managers | 1Password, Bitwarden | Secure credential storage, emergency access | Death verification, legal coordination, family document workflow |
| Legacy platforms | Trustworthy, Everplans | Document organization, family sharing | Verification/release infrastructure, partner API, professional channel |
| Big tech | Google Inactive Account Manager, Apple Digital Legacy | Free, simple | Cross-institution orchestration, legal acceptance, concierge |
| Crypto inheritance | Casa, Unchained, Vault12, Inheriti, Coincover | Crypto-native custody/recovery | Family-first, documents, no seed storage, professional channel |
| Legal/probate | Solicitors, will-writers, probate firms | Trust, legal authority | Digital vault, verification, beneficiary UX |
| Banks/insurers | Beneficiary/claims teams | Institutional trust | Cross-institution view, family coordination |

**Differentiation:** do not compete on storage. Compete on **verified release, legal acceptance, family experience, and partner distribution**.

## 3. Ideal customer profiles (ICP)

### ICP 1 — The Family Organiser (primary)

- Age 40–65, parent, non-technical, has a will or is planning one.
- Worried about family chaos, not crypto.
- Buys through a solicitor, will-writer, insurer, or employer benefit.
- Willing to pay $99–199/year for peace of mind.
- Needs concierge help to complete setup.

### ICP 2 — The Crypto/Asset Holder (premium)

- Age 30–50, technically literate, holds $50k+ in crypto/investments.
- Fears losing access or exposing secrets.
- Willing to pay $199–499/year for partner-led continuity.
- Buys through exchanges, wallet vendors, crypto advisers, or community.

### ICP 3 — The SME/Business Owner (high ACV)

- 5–200 employees, key-person risk, multiple financial accounts.
- Needs role-based continuity (CFO/COO/board).
- Willing to pay $499–2,499/year.
- Buys through accountants, solicitors, insurers, or business networks.

### ICP 4 — The Professional Partner (channel)

- Solicitor, will-writer, estate planner, insurer, broker, accountant.
- Wants a digital continuity service under their brand.
- Needs client management, status visibility, referral tracking, and compliance support.
- Buys a platform fee + per-client/verification pricing.

## 4. Positioning and messaging

### Positioning statement

For families who want to protect the people they love, LifeProof is the continuity platform that verifies when something has happened and safely releases the right access to the right people — unlike password managers, crypto wallets, or generic cloud storage, LifeProof coordinates the human, legal, and verification process, not just the files.

### Messaging pillars

1. **Peace of mind, not complexity.** “A digital safe your family can open when they need it most.”
2. **Verified release.** “We don’t just store it. We verify when it’s time and release it safely.”
3. **Trusted by professionals.** “Recommended by solicitors, insurers, and advisers.”
4. **Your secrets stay yours.** “We never store seed phrases or private keys.”
5. **Built to last.** “A continuity trust and open recovery plan, so your family is protected even if we’re not around.”

### Proof points

- Independent security audits and penetration tests.
- Open-source recovery client.
- Clear release process with cooling-off and legal hold.
- No plaintext secret storage.
- Insurance-backed service (Phase 2).
- Partner testimonials and case studies.

### Brand note

“LifeProof” is an OtterBox trademark. Complete a trademark search in the launch jurisdiction(s) and decide whether to rebrand before investing in brand equity. Have a shortlist ready.

## 5. Product ladder and pricing

Pricing must be validated with design partners before launch. Use local currency and PPP adjustments.

| Plan | Target | Price (indicative) | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free — Family Emergency Plan** | Acquisition | $0 | 3 items, 1 beneficiary, readiness score, emergency checklist, upgrade prompts |
| **Family** | Core | $99–199/year or $12–19/month | Unlimited items, 5 members, 500MB documents, 1 trusted contact, emergency pack, annual review, email support |
| **Legacy** | Crypto/asset holders | $199–499/year | Family + crypto continuity module (Phase 2), priority support, release rehearsal, 2 trusted contacts |
| **Business** | SMEs | $499–2,499/year | 20 members, role-based access, business continuity, audit export, priority support, onboarding |
| **Concierge** | HNW/families | $999+ setup; $1,500+ release | Done-for-you setup, dedicated adviser, release coordination, family briefing |
| **Partner Referral** | Solicitors/insurers | Free to partner; revenue share | Referral link, client status, co-branded materials |
| **Partner White-label** | Firms/insurers | $500–2,000/month + per client | Branding, client management, admin, reporting, revenue share |
| **Partner API** | Exchanges/insurers/platforms | Platform fee + $2–10/verification | Verification/release API, webhooks, sandbox, SLA |

### Packaging rules

- The free plan must create a complete, useful emergency plan — not a crippled demo.
- The paid plan should unlock **documents, beneficiaries, release conditions, and the release workflow**.
- Crypto is an add-on, never the front door.
- Business is sold with onboarding and role-based access.
- Concierge is priced for trust, not volume.
- Partners get margin; do not compete with them on price.

## 10. Operating model

### 10.1 Legal structure

- **OpCo:** launch jurisdiction operating company (e.g., UK Ltd or Nigeria Ltd).
- **Continuity Trust/Foundation:** separate legal entity holding escrow material, source code, and recovery runbooks.
- **IP holding:** consider a separate IP entity for brand/software if raising or expanding internationally.
- **Contracts:** ToS, privacy, DPA, beneficiary terms, partner terms, revenue share, insurance.
- **Licensing:** partner with licensed solicitors/probate providers; do not provide legal advice.

### 10.2 Insurance

- Cyber liability.
- Errors & omissions (E&O) / professional indemnity.
- Crime/fidelity.
- Directors & officers.
- Technology E&O.
- Optional: release guarantee/insurance-backed service (Phase 2).

### 10.3 Compliance

- GDPR/UK GDPR/NDPR; DPIA; records of processing; subprocessor list.
- KYC/AML/sanctions via partner; AML policy if required.
- SOC 2 Type II (Phase 2–3); ISO 27001 (Phase 3).
- Consumer protection, cooling-off, complaints.
- Data residency roadmap.
- Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA).

### 10.4 Support and concierge

| Tier | Channel | SLA | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Email/help center | 2 business days | Self-serve |
| Family | Email/chat | 1 business day | Setup, billing, plan questions |
| Legacy | Priority email/chat | 4 business hours | Crypto instructions, rehearsal |
| Business | Dedicated | 4 business hours | Onboarding, continuity |
| Concierge | Phone/video | Same day | Done-for-you setup/release |
| Release | Dedicated case manager | 24h acknowledgement | Verification/release coordination |

**Concierge principles:** empathy, plain language, no jargon, one point of contact, clear next steps, follow-up after release.

### 10.5 Operations (verification/release)

- Dedicated Release Manager and Verification Analyst(s).
- SOP, evidence standards, SLAs, escalation matrix.
- Four-eyes decisions, legal hold, fraud checks.
- Weekly QA sample of cases.
- Monthly incident/lessons review.
- Quarterly DR/tabletop.

### 10.6 Finance

- Stripe (global) + Paystack/Flutterwave (Nigeria).
- Revenue recognition: subscriptions ratable; concierge on delivery; partner fees monthly.
- Tax/VAT by jurisdiction; invoices compliant.
- Monthly close, budget vs actual, cohort analysis.
- FX management for multi-currency.

- Store seed phrases or private keys.
